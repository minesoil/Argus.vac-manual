Nut dimensions reference: https://www.accu.co.uk/hexagon-nuts/766459-NUT203M3

ASSEMBLY
1. Clean the side opening and slide the M3 nut into its pocket until its hole aligns with the central screw hole.
2. Test-fit the wheel over the hex. Do not force it or use the screw to pull an excessively tight hex into place.
3. Seat the wheel against the support flange, covering the nut-loading opening.
4. Pass the M3 screw through the wheel and thread it into the captive nut. Tighten gently.

SCREW-LENGTH CHECK
For nominal full engagement through the 2.4 mm nut, the screw should project at least 4.1 mm beyond the wheel's inner screw-hole surface into its hex recess.
For an 8 mm screw, this corresponds to at most 3.9 mm between the underside of its seated head and that inner surface, including any washer.
The actual wheel thickness is unknown, so fit of the 8 mm screw has not been confirmed.
If it cannot reach fully through the nut, use a suitable longer M3 screw after checking that it will not bottom out.
The hub has 8.3 mm of screw-tip clearance measured inward from its hex tip. This clearance is separate from the wider bottom bore.

VALIDATION
The STEP reopens as two valid solids. The nominal wheel recess, nut insertion path, and resistance to nut rotation were checked geometrically.
Printed fit, retention strength, wheel clearance against the gearbox housing, and load capacity require a physical test. The wheel's overall shape was not supplied.
