## ppt/slides/slide25.xml
The Solution
Computer Vision Integration: Runs a lightweight TensorFlow Lite machine learning model using a front-facing webcam.
Proactive Scanning: Scans the floor area ahead of time before the robot physically enters the space.
Low-Profile Hazard Detection: Identifies small, high-risk objects (like charging cables) that standard LIDAR or physical bump sensors usually miss or only react to after impact.
ROS Override: Temporarily overrides the ROS navigation stack to draw a dynamic "no-go" zone around the detected object for real-time path planning.

## ppt/slides/slide26.xml
How the Vacuum Works: Suction + Brushing
Suction: 
A high-speed motor spins an impeller fan at roughly 30,000 RPM, creating a low-pressure zone inside the dustbin. Outside air rushes in to equalize that pressure, carrying dust and fine debris with it.
Active brushing (new): 
A dedicated brush motor now agitates dirt and hair directly off the floor as the primary cleaning force, with the impeller fan providing secondary suction to collect what the brush lifts, rather than relying on suction alone.
Why the change matters: 
Suction alone struggles with debris that is stuck to or embedded in carpet fibers. Mechanical agitation from a brush roller physically dislodges it first, which is how most commercial vacuums work, and is a lesson I learned by testing suction-only performance first.

## ppt/slides/slide29.xml
Recent Fixes
Design 
change
: from suction-only to active brushing.
Original design relied entirely on impeller suction, following the San Ace 40 fan approach from the first prototype.
Testing showed suction alone was inconsistent on embedded dirt and hair, matching the "weak suction" risk I had already flagged as an anticipated challenge in the design phase.
Solution: added a dedicated brush motor to physically agitate and lift debris, with suction 
not
 working alongside it rather than doing all the work alone. This mirrors how commercial vacuums combine agitation and suction.
Hardware swaps made during the build.
Raspberry Pi 4 to Orange Pi 5 (8GB) for more processing headroom for the AI model
Raspberry Pi Camera Module to a standard USB webcam for simpler wiring and driver support on the Orange Pi

LZ-string Compression
Before any of the qr codes are generated, all the TSV(tab

 values) are compressed into Base64 format

Character Sanitization
